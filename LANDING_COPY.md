## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No login, no credit card, no catch. You get the full audit instantly.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every number traces back to an official vendor pricing page, verified
during the week of submission. AI tool pricing changes frequently —
we note the verification date on every figure.

**Q: Why do you ask for my email?**
A: We don't — until after you've seen your results. Email is optional
and only asked so we can send you the report and notify you when
better options appear for your stack.

**Q: Does this work for teams or just individuals?**
A: Both. Enter your number of seats per tool and your team size —
the audit accounts for per-seat pricing and team plan thresholds.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude,
ChatGPT Enterprise and others — sourced from companies that
overforecast or pivoted. For high-savings audits, we surface Credex
as a way to capture even more of that savings.
